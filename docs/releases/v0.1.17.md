## v0.1.17 · 当前账号核验恢复

- 修复等待已退出的核验进程时可能卡住的问题，避免阻塞后续额度刷新和账号核验。
- 启动、唤醒或 Codex 客户端重启后自动识别当前账号。短暂读取失败会自动重试，后端进程更换后会重新核验身份。
- 账号列表显示各自的登录状态。当前账号识别失败时，面板会说明原因并提供「重新核验」入口。

支持 Apple Silicon（arm64）和 macOS 14 或更高版本。可通过「连接与更新」升级，也可下载 DMG 手动安装。应用使用 ad-hoc 签名。

## English

- Fixes a wait for an exited verification process that could block quota refreshes and account checks.
- Automatically checks the active account after startup, wake, or a Codex restart. Retries temporary read failures and rechecks the identity when the backend process changes.
- Displays each account's sign-in status. If the active account cannot be identified, the panel shows the reason and offers a retry.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. The app is ad-hoc signed.
