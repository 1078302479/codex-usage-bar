# CodexUsageBar v0.1.6（开发版 / Development）

## 中文

- 新增可选的双账号监测：A 沿用本机 Codex 登录，B 通过官方网页独立登录，不覆盖 A 的认证。
- 两个账号使用独立连接、状态目录、缓存、错误恢复和额度核验；显示设置与更新间隔共用。
- 双账号面板使用等宽 A/B 卡片横向对比短期/长期额度与重置时间。点击卡片后，下方切换所选账号的重置卡、Token 统计与热力图；单账号版式保留。
- 第二个菜单栏状态项使用 `B` 前缀；顶部刷新同时核验两个账号，也可单独刷新 B。
- 支持取消登录、登录失败处理及 10 分钟等待超时；登录期间暂停该账号的额度刷新。
- 提示同账号重复登录，额度不相加。关闭第二账号只停止监测，保留 Codex 管理的独立登录。
- B 可查看独立 Token 面板和卡片明细，暂不发送重置卡通知，其监测状态不会干扰 A 的通知。

## English

- Optional concurrent monitoring for two accounts: A follows the local Codex login and B signs in independently through the official browser flow.
- Separate connections, state directories, caches, recovery and quota validation. Display preferences and refresh intervals are shared.
- Equal A/B quota cards for side-by-side comparison, with a selectable account detail area for reset credits and Token activity. Single-account layout is retained; the secondary menu bar item is prefixed `B`.
- Refresh both accounts from the header or refresh B independently.
- Cancel login, handle failures and expire pending login after 10 minutes. Quota refresh is paused while that account is signing in.
- Detect duplicate accounts without combining allowance. Disabling monitoring retains B's Codex-managed login.
- B exposes its own Token panel and reset-credit details, but sends no reset-credit notifications and cannot reconcile A's notifications.

## 验证 / Validation

已通过双账号合成状态/真实子进程夹具测试、原有原生回归，以及单独的 stdio 恢复测试。真实 Codex 空目录隔离已验证；本机已完成第二账号浏览器授权，并确认两个不同真实账号的额度同时展示。两账号长期后台运行、系统重启、不同 Codex 版本仍需进一步验证。

Validated with synthetic profile lifecycle tests, real subprocess fixtures, existing native regressions and a separate stdio recovery run. A clean profile was verified against the installed Codex; browser sign-in completed on the maintainer's Mac and quota for two distinct real accounts was displayed concurrently. Extended uptime, system reboot and other Codex versions require broader validation.
