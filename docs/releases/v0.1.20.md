## v0.1.20 · 潮汐界面

- 主弹窗换成「潮汐」界面。5 小时额度用「浪」展示，7 天额度用「潮」展示，液面随剩余额度变化。支持浅色、深色和跟随系统。
- 用完预测根据最近 30 分钟的用量变化估算重置前的剩余额度。样本足够后显示趋势图，也可在通知设置里开启预测提醒。
- 重置卡显示到期日期并突出临期卡片。Token 活动保留累计、单日峰值、连续活跃、最长单轮和 26 周热力图。
- 菜单栏可选双环、双色条、信号灯、堆叠文字和单行文字。设置窗口在当前屏幕居中打开，并适配可用空间。
- 应用图标统一为「潮汐双环」。更新时刷新应用包的修改时间，并将回滚备份保存为 Spotlight 不收录的格式，修复升级后仍可能显示旧图标的问题。

支持 Apple Silicon 和 macOS 14 或更高版本。已有用户可在「设置 → 连接与更新 → 立即检查更新」查看新版，下载并校验后选择「安装并重启」。

## English

- The Tide popup uses Wave and Tide cards for 5-hour and 7-day quotas. Their liquid levels track remaining allowance in light, dark, and system appearance.
- The depletion forecast uses recent quota changes to estimate the remaining allowance at reset. A chart appears when enough samples are available. Forecast notifications are opt-in.
- Reset credits show expiry dates and highlight credits nearing expiry. Token activity includes lifetime usage, the daily peak, activity streaks, the longest turn, and a 26-week heatmap.
- Choose dual rings, two-color bars, a status light, stacked text, or single-line text in the menu bar. Settings opens centered on the current display and fits the available space.
- The app uses the Tide rings icon. Updates refresh the bundle timestamp and keep rollback copies outside the Spotlight app list to prevent stale icons after upgrading.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Existing users can check for updates in Settings and choose Install and Restart after verification.
