## v0.1.16 · 禁止重复登录

- 添加备用账号时会核对身份。如果该账号已经登录，应用会拒绝添加并清除备用槽位的本地登录，保留正在使用的登录。已存在的重复备用登录也会在核验后清理。
- 当前账号由正在运行的 Codex 后端对应的登录目录直接核验，额度数据加载期间也能确认。

支持 Apple Silicon（arm64）和 macOS 14 或更高版本。可通过「连接与更新」升级，也可下载 DMG 手动安装。应用使用 ad-hoc 签名。

## English

- Checks the identity of a newly added backup account. If the account is already signed in, the app rejects the duplicate and clears the backup slot's local login while preserving the active login. Previously saved duplicate backup logins are also cleared after verification.
- Determines the current login from the running Codex backend's login directory, without waiting for quota data.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. The app is ad-hoc signed.
