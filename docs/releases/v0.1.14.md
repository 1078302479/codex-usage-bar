## v0.1.14 · 自动更新备份清理

- 应用内更新成功后只保留刚替换下来的上一版本，并清理旧的自动更新备份。备份保留 72 小时。若到期时应用已退出，下次启动时清理。
- 更新替换或重新启动失败时，更新器仍会尝试恢复上一版本。
- 修复使用 Swift 6.1 构建时，低额度提醒阈值界面可能崩溃的问题。

支持 Apple Silicon（arm64）和 macOS 14 或更高版本。可通过「连接与更新」升级，也可下载 DMG 手动安装。应用使用 ad-hoc 签名。

## English

- After a successful in-app update, only the immediately preceding version is kept for rollback. Backups expire after 72 hours and are removed on the next launch if the app was closed at expiry.
- If the replacement or relaunch fails, the updater still attempts to restore the previous version.
- Fixes a potential crash in the low-quota alert threshold control in Swift 6.1 builds.

Requires macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. The app is ad-hoc signed.
